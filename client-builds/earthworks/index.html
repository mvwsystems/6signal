From raw ground to finished slab

The estimate and the finished grade come from the same model.

Quantities are built with digital estimating software before a machine moves. The same design surface then loads into GPS machine control on the grading equipment, so cut and fill on the ground match the takeoff, and the pad is ready for forms when the dirt crew leaves.

That is the point of keeping dirt and concrete in one company. No second mobilization, no argument over who owns the subgrade, and one phone number for the whole scope.

How a job runs

Coordinating with the client is the job, not an extra.

  1. 01

    Site visit and takeoff

    We walk the site, pull the plans, and build the quantities in estimating software. You get a line-item proposal, not a lump sum with a question mark in it.

  2. 02

    Mobilize and clear

    Our own trucks move the equipment in. Clearing, stripping and erosion control go in before any grade is cut.

  3. 03

    Grade to the model

    GPS machine control holds the design surface. Cut, fill and compaction are checked against the same file the estimate came from.

  4. 04

    Form, pour, finish

    Foundations, slabs, paving, curb and gutter and sidewalks poured by our concrete crew on the pad the dirt crew built.

  5. 05

    Closeout

    Final grade, cleanup and a walk-through with the owner or GC. Every project is meant to meet or exceed what was promised at the start.

Before you call

Questions we get on the first call.

Does Earth Works handle both the dirt work and the concrete on a project?

Yes. Earth Works clears, grades and builds the pad, then forms and pours the foundation, paving, curb and gutter or sidewalks. One contractor carries the site from raw ground to finished concrete.

What areas does Earth Works serve?

Earth Works is based at 5939 E Hwy 62 in Lubbock, Texas and serves Lubbock and the surrounding areas of the South Plains.

How does Earth Works estimate a job?

A site visit, then quantities built with digital estimating software. Grading equipment runs GPS machine control, so the estimate and the finished grade come from the same model.

Can Earth Works haul equipment and materials?

Yes. Earth Works runs its own transport fleet for machinery, large loads and construction materials in and around Lubbock.
