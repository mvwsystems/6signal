# 6Signal White Paper Cover Series — Creative Direction

Master reference for the Research white paper cover series. Covers 01–03 exist
and have been visually verified against the actual artwork:

- **01 / 03 — The Question Cluster Advantage** (dark; hub-and-spoke cluster diagram)
- **02 / 03 — The Local Entity Gap** (dark; coordinate dot-field with glowing unresolved entity)
- **03 / 03 — Named, Not Just Found** (light cream stock; tagged **FIELD MANUAL**, not WHITE PAPER; entity record card, yellow spine bar, punch holes, stamp)

Series numbering is by design order, not publication date. Covers 04–07 are
specified in the four prompt files in this folder; their tag is WHITE PAPER
and their denominators are / 07. Do not renumber or regenerate 01–03 without
explicit approval.

## Shared visual rules (never vary)

- Predominantly near-black field (#060606–#0b0b0a), except designated light
  covers (see rhythm, below), which use warm off-white technical-document stock
  (#f2f1ec ± a step).
- One accent only: acid/signal yellow **#E6FF00**. Status/diagram grays for
  everything secondary. No blue, purple, red, or rainbow gradients — ever.
- SIXSIGNAL lockup near the top. Content-type label **WHITE PAPER**. Series
  number in NN / 07 form. A FIELD OF STUDY classification line.
- Small monospaced metadata, wide-tracked eyebrows, thin rules, registration
  marks, faint grids or coordinates — the intelligence-briefing apparatus.
- One dominant conceptual diagram per cover, tied directly to the paper's
  thesis. No decoration without meaning.
- High negative space. Editorial, not promotional. Proprietary field-research
  feeling.
- Restrained glow: yellow may bloom slightly at the single most important
  diagram state, never across the composition.

## What may vary (and must)

- Composition architecture: headline top vs. middle, diagram dominant vs.
  supporting, vertical vs. horizontal reading axis. The series varies
  structurally while remaining related — do not template.
- Background: the series breathes via occasional light covers (03 is light;
  05 is the designated light cover of the new group).
- Diagram vocabulary: each paper owns its own diagram type (matrix, stack,
  gate sequence, selection field). Never reuse a prior cover's diagram.
- Italic emphasis in titles: selective, compositional, optional.

## Title signature (verified on all three existing covers — mandatory)

The headline is uppercase, and **the final word is set in #E6FF00 italic and
ends with a period**: "THE QUESTION CLUSTER *ADVANTAGE.*", "THE LOCAL ENTITY
*GAP.*", "NAMED, NOT JUST *FOUND.*" New covers follow suit: *PLAYBOOK.* /
*BLUEPRINT.* / *REPORT.* / *GAP.* (07's echo of 02 is intentional — bookend
papers; their diagrams must differ completely.)

## Verified layout apparatus (from the actual covers)

- Top-left: SIXSIGNAL lockup with triple-chevron mark. Top-right:
  "● [TAG] · NN / NN" mono, with a small yellow dot bullet.
- Eyebrow: short #E6FF00 dash, then "FIELD OF STUDY · […]" wide-tracked mono.
- Subtitle: monospaced, sentence case, 2 lines max.
- Footer: three-column strip above a hairline — AUTHOR (name + a two-part
  category sub-line, e.g. "AI Visibility · Service Contractors") | PUBLISHED
  (Month Year) | READ (URL + " →", often in accent).
- Light covers add: full-height #E6FF00 spine bar at the left edge, punch-hole
  marks, corner registration crosses, optional classification strip and stamp.

## Typography hierarchy

1. **Headline** — large industrial/geometric sans, white (or near-black on the
   light cover), tight leading, uppercase, with the title signature above.
   Project display face: Chakra Petch (or closest geometric equivalent —
   do not name protected commercial fonts).
2. **Eyebrow / classification** — monospaced (JetBrains Mono or closest),
   uppercase, tracking ≈ +0.2em, small, gray or accent.
3. **Subtitle/descriptor** — humanist sans (Inter or closest), off-white/gray,
   sentence case, restrained size.
4. **Metadata block** — monospaced, small, gray; accent only for the series
   number or one keyword.
5. Diagram labels — monospaced, uppercase, small; one highlighted label may be
   accent yellow.

## Metadata conventions (every cover)

- SIXSIGNAL brand lockup (top area)
- WHITE PAPER · NN / 07
- FIELD OF STUDY · [CLASSIFICATION]
- Full title (+ subtitle/descriptor from frontmatter description)
- Author line: The 6Signal Research Team
- Research category + Published [Month Year]
- Read indicator (e.g., 38 MIN READ) and/or restrained URL: 6signal.co/research/[slug]

## URL convention — open decision

Existing covers print short URLs: `6signal.co/papers/01 →` (and `/manual` for
03). Those routes may not exist on the site. The new prompts default to the
real URLs (`6signal.co/research/[slug] →`). If the short `/papers/NN` style is
preferred for visual consistency, add redirects `/papers/04..07 →
/research/[slug]` and swap the READ line in each prompt.

## Series rhythm (default direction for 04–07)

04 dark / measurement instrument · **05 light / infrastructure schematic** ·
06 dark / qualification sequence · 07 dark / selection field. Across the full
seven: two light covers (03, 05) spaced among five dark — deliberate rhythm.
05 inherits 03's light-stock devices (yellow spine, punch holes, registration)
so the two light covers read as siblings.

## Export (verified against actual artwork: ~2040 × 2630, ratio ≈ 1 : 1.288)

- **2040 × 2630 px portrait** — matches the existing series files
  (2044×2633 / 2032×2617 / 2041×2629).
- Full-bleed artwork, no mockups, no device frames, no workspace chrome.
- Safe margins: keep all text ≥ 120 px from every edge (≈ 6% of width).
- Web-ready PNG, sharp text at 100%.

## File naming
