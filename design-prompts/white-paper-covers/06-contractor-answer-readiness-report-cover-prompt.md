## 3. Visual concept

A **qualification sequence** — a field assessment in progress. The paper's argument: ranking is not enough; before an engine names a contractor, the business passes through verification gates (service match, location match, trust, proof, availability), and only the business that clears every gate becomes the answer. The cover shows several anonymous contractor entities (abstract marks/nodes — never trucks, hard hats, or tools) entering an evaluation sequence drawn as a vertical or horizontal chain of checkpoint stages with mono-labeled criteria. Most entities stall at a gate, marked NOT RESOLVED in gray. **One entity clears the full sequence and locks in acid yellow — ANSWER-READY, SELECTED.** The emotional register is evaluative and tactical: a readiness assessment sheet from a field operation, the moment of a signal-lock.

## 6. Diagram system

A gated sequence: entity marks (small squares or hex nodes, deliberately anonymous) on entry lines; four-to-five checkpoint stages as thin ruled frames, each carrying one criterion tag; failed entities exit as dimmed lines tagged NOT RESOLVED; one continuous #E6FF00 line threads every gate and terminates in a filled yellow state block labeled ANSWER-READY with a small SELECTED tag. Registration marks, faint index numerals at stages (01–05), hairline connective rules. Labels drawn ONLY from: SERVICE MATCH · LOCATION MATCH · TRUST VERIFIED · PROOF PRESENT · AVAILABLE · ANSWER-READY · NOT RESOLVED · SELECTED · FINDABLE. (FINDABLE may tag the entry field to contrast with ANSWER-READY at the exit — the paper's core distinction.)
