## The six signals we check

The name 6Signal is not decoration.

We look at the core signals that determine whether a contractor is findable, credible, and conversion-ready.

### 1. Website clarity

The first question is simple:

Can a visitor understand what you do within five seconds?

We check:

- Homepage headline
- Subheadline
- Main CTA
- Service clarity
- Location clarity
- Trust proof
- Mobile layout
- Navigation
- Page speed experience
- Visual credibility

A website can look good and still be unclear.

Clarity comes first.

### 2. Local SEO strength

Next, we look at how well your company is positioned for local searches.

We check:

- Google Business Profile
- Local rankings
- Service-area relevance
- NAP consistency
- City pages
- Service pages
- Local backlinks or mentions
- Category selection
- Review volume and quality

Local SEO is not just "ranking."

It is whether the market can connect your company to the right service in the right place.

### 3. AI visibility

AI visibility is becoming the next layer of search.

We check whether your company is likely to be understood, cited, or recommended by AI search systems.

That includes:

- Clear entity signals
- Strong service descriptions
- Helpful FAQ content
- Third-party mentions
- Review specificity
- Authoritative pages
- Local relevance
- Structured content
- Consistent company information

The question is:

If someone asks AI for a contractor in your category and market, are you likely to appear?

If not, why not?

### 4. Conversion flow

Traffic does not matter if people do not act.

We check:

- CTA strength
- Form clarity
- Phone visibility
- Booking path
- Quote/inspection request flow
- Emergency call path
- Trust placement
- Friction points
- Mobile conversion
- Follow-up expectations

The visitor should never wonder what to do next.

### 5. Reputation and proof

Contractors sell trust.

So we look at the proof.

We check:

- Google reviews
- Review recency
- Review specificity
- Testimonials
- Project photos
- Case studies
- Before-and-after examples
- Licenses and certifications
- Warranty language
- Team credibility
- Social proof

If your company is excellent offline but weak online, your website is underselling you.

### 6. Revenue leakage

This is where most contractors miss money.

We look for places where opportunities are leaking out of the system.

Common issues include:

- Missed calls
- Slow follow-up
- No text-back system
- Weak form routing
- No CRM
- No estimate follow-up
- No review automation
- No reactivation campaign
- No abandoned lead process
- No visibility tracking

Sometimes the fastest win is not more traffic.

Sometimes it is capturing what you already have.

## Example findings

Here are common findings from contractor audits:

### Finding 1: The homepage is too vague

The company may be credible, but the homepage says nothing specific.

Fix:

Rewrite the hero section, clarify services, strengthen CTA, and show service areas immediately.

### Finding 2: The service pages are too thin

The company offers valuable services, but the pages have almost no useful content.

Fix:

Build deeper service pages with FAQs, process, proof, location relevance, and conversion sections.

### Finding 3: Reviews are strong but underused

The company has great reviews, but the website barely shows them.

Fix:

Add review sections, service-specific testimonials, and review language near CTAs.

### Finding 4: Calls are being missed

The company is paying for traffic but losing phone opportunities.

Fix:

Install missed-call text-back, call tracking, and faster lead routing.

### Finding 5: AI visibility is weak

The company has no content that clearly explains its services, process, location, or expertise.

Fix:

Create AI-readable service pages, FAQ content, comparison content, and third-party visibility.
