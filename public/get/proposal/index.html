Prompt
Sources attached: the RFP, our last three winning proposals, the project list, and résumés. Draft Section {n} for {project}:

1 Project approach: restate the owner's stated priorities from the RFP in order, then our approach to each, one paragraph per priority, specific to this site and schedule.
2 Team: org chart text and a 60-word bio per key person, drawn from résumés; pair each person with the RFP's stated evaluation criteria.
3 Relevant experience: pick the five most comparable projects from the list by type, size and owner; 80 words each with value, dates, role, and one measurable outcome.
4 Schedule narrative: milestones from the RFP, our sequencing logic, the two risks and how they are controlled.
5 Safety: EMR, incident rates and the three program elements most relevant to this project's hazards.

Match the tone of our past proposals. No superlatives. Flag every claim that needs a number we have not provided. Finish with a compliance matrix: RFP requirement → section and page where it is answered.
