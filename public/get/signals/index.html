The six signals
SignalWhat it means for a contractor
GEOGenerative Engine Optimization: when someone asks ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or Perplexity for a contractor in your trade and city, is your firm one of the names in the answer?
AEOAnswer Engine Optimization: do Google’s AI Overviews and other direct answers cite a page of yours?
LEOLocal Entity Optimization: do Google Maps, Apple Maps, Yelp, BBB and the directories all show the same name, address, phone, categories and hours?
VEOVoice Engine Optimization: when someone asks Siri, Alexa or Google Assistant for your trade near them, does your firm come back?
PEOPrompt Engine Optimization: do you surface for the exact phrases buyers type and say ("emergency roof repair", "best commercial GC in {city}"), not only your company name?
IEOIndex Engine Optimization: can AI crawlers reach and read your site: schema, clean service pages, and a robots.txt that lets GPTBot, ClaudeBot and PerplexityBot in?
