Template · paste in <head>
<script type="application/ld+json">
{
  "@context": "https://schema.org",
  "@type": "GeneralContractor",
  "name": "{Firm legal name}",
  "url": "https://{domain}",
  "logo": "https://{domain}/logo.png",
  "image": "https://{domain}/hq.jpg",
  "telephone": "{+1-xxx-xxx-xxxx}",
  "email": "{contact@domain}",
  "address": {"@type": "PostalAddress", "streetAddress": "{street}", "addressLocality": "{city}", "addressRegion": "{state}", "postalCode": "{zip}", "addressCountry": "US"},
  "geo": {"@type": "GeoCoordinates", "latitude": {lat}, "longitude": {lng}},
  "areaServed": [{"@type": "City", "name": "{city 1}"}, {"@type": "City", "name": "{city 2}"}],
  "foundingDate": "{YYYY}",
  "numberOfEmployees": {"@type": "QuantitativeValue", "value": {count}},
  "knowsAbout": ["{design-build}", "{tenant improvement}", "{tilt-up}", "{healthcare construction}"],
  "hasOfferCatalog": {"@type": "OfferCatalog", "name": "Services", "itemListElement": [{"@type": "Offer", "itemOffered": {"@type": "Service", "name": "{Service 1}"}}]},
  "sameAs": ["{GBP URL}", "{LinkedIn URL}", "{AGC/ABC member page}", "{YouTube channel}"],
  "aggregateRating": {"@type": "AggregateRating", "ratingValue": "{4.8}", "reviewCount": "{37}"}
}
</script>

Validate at validator.schema.org, then in Google Search Console → Enhancements. Remove aggregateRating if you have fewer than five public reviews.

Every field, and the signal it feeds

The block as a whole is IEO: it is how crawlers parse the firm. Each field also feeds the signals below.

FieldWhat to put thereSignal
name, url, logo, imageLegal firm name exactly as on your GBP; homepage URL; square logo and an HQ or crew photoIEO · GEO
telephone, email, addressThe same NAP as your GBP footer, character for characterLEO · VEO
geoLatitude and longitude of the office (the generator can look it up)LEO · VEO
areaServedThe cities you actually work in, one per entryLEO · GEO
foundingDate, numberOfEmployeesYear founded and headcount: signals of an established firmGEO
knowsAboutDelivery methods, building types and specialties, written the way buyers say themPEO · GEO
hasOfferCatalogOne Service per line item you sellAEO · PEO
sameAsGBP, LinkedIn, association member page, YouTube channelLEO · GEO
aggregateRatingOnly if you have five or more public reviews; match the GBP numbersLEO

Validation: paste the block at validator.schema.org, fix every error, then check Google Search Console → Enhancements a week after publishing.
