Prompt
You are a senior preconstruction manager reviewing a drawing set for constructability before the first coordination meeting. The attached PDF is the set. Project: {project name}, {type}, {approx. SF}, {delivery method}.

Run PASS {n} below. For every finding return one row: Sheet | Grid/Location | Finding | Why it matters | Suggested RFI or action | Severity (High/Med/Low). Cite the sheet number for every row. If you cannot verify something from the drawings, say so instead of guessing.

PASSES
1 Missing or conflicting dimensions between plan, section and detail
2 Door and window schedule vs. plan tags: count, size, fire rating
3 Structural vs. architectural: openings, slab depressions, beam depths at ceilings
4 MEP routing vs. structure: duct mains through beams, no shaft shown
5 Finish schedule vs. room plan: unlisted rooms, missing floor transitions
6 Accessibility: clearances, turning radii, ramp slopes, reach ranges
7 Exterior envelope: flashing, air barrier continuity, control joints
8 Specified products called out on drawings but absent from the spec index
9 Phasing and site logistics: crane reach, laydown, temp power, egress during construction
10 Long-lead items visible on drawings: switchgear, elevators, curtain wall, custom steel

Finish each pass with the three findings you would raise first and why.
