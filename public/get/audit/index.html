Prompt
Run each prompt in ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity and Google (look for the AI Overview). Record for each: NAMED (your firm appears), CITED (a page of yours is the source), or ABSENT.

1 Who are the best {trade} contractors in {city}?
2 Recommend a general contractor for a {project type} in {city}.
3 Is {your firm} a reputable contractor?
4 What does {your firm} specialize in?
5 How much does a {project type} cost per square foot in {city}?
6 Which contractors in {city} have experience with {building type, e.g. medical office}?
7 Who should I call for a {service, e.g. design-build warehouse} near {city}?
8 Compare {your firm} and {competitor}.
9 What do reviews say about {your firm}?
10 Which {city} contractors are members of {AGC chapter or association}?

Scoring: NAMED = 1, CITED = 2, ABSENT = 0. Forty prompt-engine pairs; maximum 80. Under 20: invisible. 20–45: emerging. Over 45: visible.
