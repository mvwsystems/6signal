Scoring
How to read it0–4: late curve, 18+ months behind. 5–8: middle, 6–12 months. 9–12: leader curve.
Three bands
0–4 · Late curve · 18+ months behindOn site: Reports typed at night from memory. RFIs answered from a blank field. A few people use free ChatGPT on their phones, with no policy and nobody responsible.
First move: Write a one-page AI use policy, put every PM on a business-plan assistant seat with data controls on, and start with meeting notes → action items.
5–8 · Middle curve · 6–12 months behindOn site: Pockets of real use. One or two champions. Nothing is measured, nothing connects to Procore or ACC, and results depend on who is on the job.
First move: Name one owner, pick one workflow (daily reports or RFI drafts), and measure 30 days before and after. Make the result the standard on every job.
9–12 · Leader curve · Setting the paceOn site: Workflows run with owners and review steps. A monthly review retires what nobody uses. Estimating and safety draw on the firm’s own data.
First move: Wire the running workflows into project systems through an automation layer, and track your six-signal visibility score every quarter.
