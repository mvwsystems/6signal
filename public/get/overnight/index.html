Overnight Shift Blueprint

Five automations that run between 5 PM and 6 AM so the superintendent opens a finished list, not a blank one.

Download PDF
What you get
  • Five automations, each as trigger → steps → output
  • Tool pairings for Procore/ACC + Google Workspace or Microsoft 365
  • Build order: easiest first
Build steps
  1. 11:00 PM — Daily report compile. Trigger: schedule. Pull today's manpower log, delivered materials, weather, and photos from Procore/ACC. Assistant drafts the report in your template. Output: draft in the super's inbox for a 6 AM sign-off.
  2. 11:30 PM — Open RFI digest. Query RFIs with no response in 5+ days. Assistant writes one chase email per responsible party with the question restated and the schedule impact. Output: drafts, not sends.
  3. 12:00 AM — Two-week lookahead. Read the schedule export; list activities starting in 14 days that have no submittal approved or no material delivered. Output: a flagged list for the PM.
  4. 4:30 AM — Weather and crew. Pull tomorrow's hourly forecast; if wind, rain or heat thresholds are crossed, draft the crane, concrete or heat-stress notice from your own templates.
  5. 5:30 AM — Inbox pre-sort. Tag overnight email by project and by risk words (delay, extra, backcharge, revised, hold). Output: a short morning brief, highest risk first.
