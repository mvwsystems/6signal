Change-Order Risk Inbox Triage

Every incoming project email gets read for change-order language and tagged. The ones that cost money rise to the top of the morning.

Download PDF
What you get
  • Gmail or Outlook rules plus one assistant classification step
  • Risk vocabulary list you can extend
  • Daily digest format
Build steps
  1. Create a label/category per project and one called CO-RISK.
  2. Rule: any email from a project contact or with the project number in the subject gets the project label.
  3. Automation (Zapier/Make/n8n or Power Automate): on new labeled email, send subject + body to the assistant with the classification prompt.
  4. Assistant returns: risk level (none/low/high), the trigger phrase, and a one-line reason.
  5. High → apply CO-RISK, star it, add to a sheet with date, sender, phrase, and the notice deadline from your contract (e.g. 7 days).
  6. 7:00 AM digest email: all CO-RISK items from the last 24 hours, oldest deadline first.
Prompt inside the automation
Prompt inside the automation
Classify this construction project email for change-order or claim risk. Risk phrases include: extra, additional, not in our scope, revised, delay, impact, backcharge, hold, differing condition, unforeseen, direction to proceed, acceleration, time extension, notice. Return JSON: {"risk":"none|low|high","phrase":"…","reason":"one sentence","suggested_action":"reply / log notice / call PM"}. High = money or time is being claimed or directed. Low = language suggests it may come.
