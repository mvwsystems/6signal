Prompt
Prompt
Attached: the transcript of the {project} OAC meeting on {date}, and last meeting's minutes. Produce minutes in this structure: Attendees | Safety | Schedule (milestones, two-week lookahead, delays) | Submittals and RFIs | Change orders and budget | Quality | Owner items | Old business (carry forward each open item from last minutes with status: open / closed / updated) | New action items.

Action items as a table: # | Item | Owner (name, company) | Due date | Transcript timestamp. If an owner or date was not stated, write TBD and list it under "Needs assignment". Neutral tone, no interpretation of intent, no quotes longer than one sentence. Under 900 words.
