6 AM Punch Digest

Every open punch item, grouped by responsible sub, written into one email per sub, sent before the gate opens.

Download PDF
What you get
  • Seven steps from Procore export to sent email
  • The assistant prompt that writes each sub email
  • What to measure: days-open before and after
Build steps
  1. Nightly 9 PM: export open punch items (Procore Punch List or ACC Issues) to a sheet — item, location, trade, responsible company, due date, days open, photo link.
  2. Group rows by responsible company.
  3. For each group, send the rows to the assistant with the email prompt below.
  4. Assistant returns a subject line and a body: items sorted by location, oldest first, with photo links, and one sentence on tomorrow's access window.
  5. Save as drafts to the super's account (not auto-send for the first two weeks).
  6. 5:45 AM: super reviews and sends; or after two clean weeks, auto-send at 5:45 AM.
  7. Weekly: chart average days-open per sub. Share it at the OAC.
Prompt inside the automation
Prompt inside the automation
Write a short email to {sub company} from {GC name} listing their open punch items on {project}. Group by floor and room, oldest first. For each item: location, one-line description, days open, photo link. End with tomorrow's access window: {window}. Plain tone, no exclamation marks, under 180 words plus the list.
