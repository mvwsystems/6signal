Prompt
Attached: a photo of a delivery ticket and our open PO list (CSV: PO number, vendor, item, qty ordered, qty received, unit price, cost code). Extract: Vendor | Ticket number | Date | Items (description, qty, unit) | Received by (name/signature present yes/no) | Truck/plant if shown. Mark any field you are not confident about with "?".

Match to a PO by vendor and item. Return a ledger line: Date | Vendor | PO | Item | Qty | Unit price | Extended | Cost code | Status (Matched / Over PO / No PO / Needs review). Explain the status in one line.
