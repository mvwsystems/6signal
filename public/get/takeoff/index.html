Prompt
Attached: the architectural finish plan(s) and finish schedule for {project}. Build a complete room finish table with columns: Room number | Room name | Floor finish | Base | Wall finish (N/E/S/W if different) | Ceiling finish | Ceiling height | Area SF (from plan dimensions; mark estimated) | Notes/conflicts.

Include every room shown on the plan, including unlabeled closets and corridors. Where the schedule and the plan tag disagree, write both and flag it. Then total SF by floor finish type.

Second pass: here is my takeoff {paste}. List every room where our quantities differ by more than 5% or where a finish differs.
