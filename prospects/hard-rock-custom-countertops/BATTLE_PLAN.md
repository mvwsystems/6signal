## 1. The read in one page

Hard Rock is a fifteen-year, 4.9-to-5.0-rated stone fabricator that the internet thinks is a
two-year-old company with eight reviews. That gap is the whole opportunity.

- **Identity is split.** The business rebranded from *Hard Rock Granite & Tile, Inc.* to *Hard
  Rock Custom Countertops*, moved from Dallas to Arlington, then opened a Southlake office. The old
  domain is still live with its own pages and a different email. Listings exist under two names and
  at four addresses (Dallas, Watauga, Arlington, Southlake). Google, Yelp, Angi, Houzz, BBB,
  Nextdoor, and D&B each hold a different fragment. AI engines resolve entities by consistency;
  this one does not resolve.
- **Proof is stranded.** Roughly 150+ five-star reviews exist across Angi (97 + 37), Houzz (7, plus
  Best of Houzz 2017 and 2019), Birdeye (8), Nextdoor, and HomeAdvisor. The surface AI engines
  weight most, the Southlake Google Business Profile, shows 8. Yelp, the source ChatGPT cites most
  for local services, shows 1 review and the wrong category ("General Contractors"). Angi's
  Arlington listing carries a "this pro may not be in business" flag.
- **They sit in the richest zip codes in Tarrant County and have no page saying so.** Southlake
  typical home value is about $1.3M, Colleyville about $1.0M and rising 11% year over year, Westlake
  median sale price $5.1M. Competitors headquartered elsewhere (Arlington Marble in Colleyville,
  Texas Counter Fitters in Richardson) have already built "Countertops Southlake TX" landing pages.
  Hard Rock, physically on S. Kimball Ave in Southlake, has none. Its indexed bathroom page is
  titled for Frisco.
- **Commercial demand is real and documented.** The Blue Book counted countertop scopes on 232
  restaurant and 76 non-residential projects in DFW in the past year. Arlington's Entertainment
  District has a $500M Loews hotel breaking ground, a $135M mixed-use project, and a luxury
  multifamily tower in motion. Fort Worth permitted $473M of multifamily in January 2026 alone.
  Hard Rock mentions "offices and commercial" in one sentence on its site and nowhere else.
- **Two market shocks favor them if they say so out loud.** The Section 201 quartz safeguard tariff
  (25% in-quota / 50% over-quota, effective Aug 15 2026, stacked on existing India/Türkiye duties)
  makes quartz quotes volatile. Natural stone is outside scope. Nobody in DFW is marketing
  "tariff-proof natural stone, quote locked for 60 days." And 2026 designer trend reports put
  quartzite (Taj Mahal), honed finishes, and porcelain at the top, which is exactly Hard Rock's
  natural-stone strength.

**Thesis for the pitch:** consolidate the entity, move the proof to where AI looks, build the three
buyer journeys they actually want (luxury homeowner, builder/designer, commercial GC), and measure
it with the probe engine every two weeks. The first big visible win is achievable inside 30 days:
Google and Yelp showing the real review volume and the right category, and the Angi "may not be in
business" flag gone.

### 3.4 Tailwinds nobody local is using

1. **Quartz tariff.** Section 201 safeguard effective Aug 15 2026: 25% within a tariff-rate quota,
   50% above it, on top of AD/CVD duties on India and Türkiye continued Jan 2026. Granite, marble,
   quartzite, soapstone are outside scope. Message: "Natural stone is tariff-proof. Your quote is
   locked 60 days." This is a differentiated, factual, timely hook for homeowners, designers, and
   GC estimators who are getting re-quoted on quartz.
2. **Silica.** Cal/OSHA is advancing emergency restrictions on high-silica engineered stone (not
   Texas, not final). Architects and national GCs are already writing silica language into specs.
   A one-page "Silica-safe fabrication statement" (wet cutting, dust control, PPE, monitoring) wins
   spec compliance conversations before competitors have one. **[verify their actual shop practice
   before publishing anything]**
3. **AI answer share.** A 2026 study found Google shows an AI answer on 88% of local-service
   searches; Google AI Mode cites GBP data in roughly 80% of local answers, AI Overviews cite the
   business's own site in roughly 73%; Yelp is ChatGPT's most consistent local citation; Reddit
   is a top-cited domain. Every one of those surfaces is weak or wrong for Hard Rock today, which is
   why the fixes compound fast.

## 4. Strategy: three buyer journeys, one entity

### 4.1 Journey A, the luxury homeowner (Southlake, Colleyville, Westlake, Keller, Trophy Club)
Asks ChatGPT/Google "best countertop fabricator near Southlake for quartzite," reads the GBP,
reads Yelp/Houzz, looks for a portfolio with their neighborhood in it. Needs: city pages with real
local jobs, a Taj Mahal/quartzite authority page, a designer-grade portfolio, consolidated reviews,
and a by-appointment Southlake experience that reads as premium, not as "no showroom."

### 4.2 Journey B, the builder / designer / showroom
Does not search. Refers. Needs: a trade program page (lead times, templating SLA, pricing sheet
they can bid from, 50/50 payment terms, dedicated PM), a lunch, and reliability proof. 6 Signal's
visibility work gives Hard Rock the credibility artifacts; the owner does the lunches. One designer
relationship can feed 5–15 jobs a month per industry sources; treat that as a hypothesis to test
with three designers in 60 days.

### 4.3 Journey C, the commercial GC / estimator
Finds subs through the Blue Book, past bid lists, ISqFt/BuildingConnected invites, and
certification directories. Searches "commercial countertop fabricator Fort Worth" when a sub drops.
Needs: a commercial page, a capability statement PDF, a bid-ready line card, shop-drawing capacity
statement, insurance/bonding facts, and, ideally, a certification that primes are required to
solicit.

### 4.4 The outside-the-box levers
1. **Texas HUB certification (if eligible).** On state-funded projects (ISDs, universities,
   hospital districts, TxDOT), primes must solicit at least three HUBs for each subcontracting
   opportunity and justify in writing any non-HUB pick. Certification is free, takes up to five
   months, lasts four years. Eligibility is ownership by minority or women owners with real
   control. The BXB directory listing suggests this may apply **[verify with the owner, do not
   assume]**. Veteran ownership does not qualify for Texas HUB on its own; check federal SDVOSB/VOSB
   via SBA and the City of Fort Worth/Arlington local programs. If any of these apply, file in the
   first 30 days: it is the one commercial lever competitors cannot copy by next quarter.
2. **Manufacturer locators are AI-cited sources.** Cambria Premier Partner, Cosentino/Silestone,
   Caesarstone, MSI, Daltile fabricator locators are crawlable, authoritative, and already cited in
   AI answers for competitors. Get listed on every program they qualify for.
3. **Tariff quote-lock offer** (above). Factual, timely, and nobody local is saying it.
4. **The Blue Book / ISqFt / BuildingConnected profiles under the new name**, with the capability
   statement attached. That is where GC estimators actually look.
5. **Reddit and Nextdoor are AI citation sources.** Owner-answered threads in r/Dallas, r/FortWorth,
   r/SouthlakeTX (if active), and Nextdoor Southlake/Colleyville on "who did your countertops" are
   cheap, durable, and cited.
6. **Stabilize rung, scoped later:** missed-call text-back and an AI receptionist for the
   (817) line matter more than usual here because the Southlake office is appointment-only; a
   luxury buyer who gets voicemail calls Arlington Marble next.
7. **Labs tie-in:** BidCore / Takeoff Copilot for commercial bid takeoffs is a natural upsell once
   they are receiving GC invitations. Do not pitch it in the first 90 days.
