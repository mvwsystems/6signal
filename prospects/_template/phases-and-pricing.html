How the pricing works

Fixed figures above are firm. Scoped phases are priced from a paid scoping session, $500 remote or $1,000 on site, credited in full against the build if you proceed within thirty days. Internal infrastructure cannot be quoted before someone has seen how the shop actually runs; a number given before that is a guess dressed as a quote.

The $1,000 Google Business Profile build stands alone if you want only that. Start the 90-Day Sprint and it is included at no extra cost. The Sprint is not discounted; the build is folded in, because a profile without posting, reviews and citations behind it decays back to where it started.

GBP Autopilot is the one recurring line in the foundation, and it is optional. The $1,000 build ships with the 52-week posting kit, so the profile can be run in-house from day one. Autopilot runs the same calendar for you, from your photos, at $97 a month: included for the ninety days of the Sprint, month to month after it, cancel any time. Nothing in the build depends on keeping it.

You shoot it, we do everything after. Photography and video are not included in any phase. You supply the images from your own camera or a photographer you hire; selection, naming, sequencing and publishing across the site, the Google profile and every channel in the plan are ours. Advertising spend, association dues, certification fees and third-party software are paid directly by you and are not fees to 6 Signal.
